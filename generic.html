About CuteminiBox

Game developer tools built with real pipelines in mind

Who We Are

CuteminiBox is an independent Unity asset publisher dedicated to building high-quality, production-ready tools for game developers. We are developers ourselves — we have felt the friction of slow level-design pipelines, the pain of rebuilding the same track systems from scratch on every project, and the frustration of assets that look great on the store page but fall apart in a real codebase.

That experience is exactly what drives us. Every asset we release is something we would want to use on our own projects — documented properly, built to last, and designed to slot into your workflow without fighting you.

What We Build

Our current catalogue focuses on race and level design tooling for Unity, with more categories in active development. We specialise in editor-time tools that save hours of manual work — the kind of tools that make you wonder how you built without them.

  • Modular track and circuit building systems with smart snapping and validation
  • Spline-based road and terrain generation with zero manual modelling
  • Compatible with Built-in, URP, and HDRP render pipelines out of the box
  • Lightweight runtime footprint — our tools do the heavy lifting at edit time
  • Clean, commented source code you can extend and maintain long-term
  • Actively maintained with regular updates and direct developer support

Our Philosophy

We believe a great asset should feel invisible. It should fit naturally into the way you already work, handle the tedious parts quietly, and get out of the way when you need to make creative decisions. We do not ship bloatware, we do not pad documentation to look more professional than we are, and we do not abandon assets after the first sale.

When you buy from CuteminiBox you get direct access to the people who built the tool. If something does not work the way you expect, we want to hear about it — and we will fix it.
